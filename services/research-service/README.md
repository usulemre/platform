# Research Service (`research_service`)

> **Phase 2.1 — Research Service (implemented).** Governs the complete lifecycle of quantitative
> research: transforms ideas into structured, traceable, reproducible research initiatives and
> orchestrates them across datasets, features, experiments, and validation workflows. **Institutional
> research model, interfaces only** — no statistical algorithms, no backtesting, no AI, no
> persistence, no infrastructure, no API, no UI.

## Purpose

Realize the **Research Intelligence Layer** (Architecture V2 §5.5). The Research Service **proposes
and records**; it **never adjudicates** statistical significance or acceptance (that is the
deterministic Validation engine — CP-5, AI-2), and it **never observes** validation/OOS outcomes
(the isolation barrier — AD-3, `P2-07`). Register-before-run and the pre-registration lock are
enforced (SM-1/2); negative results are first-class (SM-4). It reuses the Phase-1 foundations rather
than duplicating them.

## What is here (Phase 2.1)

14 modules, each a subpackage with its own `README.md`:
`model` · `lifecycle` · `status` · `metadata` · `ownership` · `classification` · `dependencies` ·
`registration` · `management` · `policies` · `specifications` · `events` · `errors` · `repositories`.

- **Canonical models** (across `model`/`metadata`/`ownership`/`classification`/`status`): `Research`
  (aggregate, reuses `core_domain.AggregateRoot`), `ResearchIdentifier`, `ResearchMetadata`,
  `ResearchObjective`, `ResearchHypothesis`, `ResearchOwner`, `ResearchStatus`,
  `ResearchClassification`, `ResearchPriority`, `ResearchEvidence`, `ResearchReference`.
- **Lifecycle**: `PROPOSED → REGISTERED → DESIGNED → ACTIVE → UNDER_REVIEW → VALIDATED → APPROVED →
ARCHIVED` (+ `SUSPENDED`), with **revisions** (`ACTIVE↔DESIGNED`, `UNDER_REVIEW→ACTIVE`),
  **suspension/reopening** (`↔SUSPENDED`), and **branching** (a reopened/killed effort creates a new
  versioned lineage, never a mutation — RL-1). Skips forbidden (fail-closed); `ARCHIVED` terminal.
- **Domain events** (`events`): `ResearchCreated`, `ResearchRegistered`, `ResearchStarted`,
  `ResearchUpdated`, `ResearchSubmittedForReview`, `ResearchValidated`, `ResearchApproved`,
  `ResearchArchived` (subclass `core_domain.shared.DomainEvent`; `ResearchValidated` _records_ a
  deterministic-engine outcome, it does not assert it).

## How it reuses the foundations (no duplication)

- **`core_domain.research`** — `FalsifiablePrediction`, `EconomicRationale`, `Hypothesis` (referenced
  by identity), `PreRegistration`, `PreRegistrationService`.
- **`core_domain.shared`** — `AggregateRoot`, `EntityId`, `Version`, `Ref`, `Provenance`,
  `DomainEvent`, `DomainError`.

## Boundary rules (verified)

- **Proposes/records, never adjudicates:** the `errors` module encodes `ResearchSelfAdjudication`
  (CP-5), `IsolationBarrierBreach` (AD-3/P2-07), and `PreRegistrationAltered` (p-hacking, SM-2/FB-8);
  `specifications` are explicitly _structural_, deferring the promotion decision to the scientific
  gate (P2-09).
- **Deterministic, technology-independent:** a code scan confirms **no** statistical/backtest/AI/
  persistence/infrastructure imports — only stdlib + `core_domain`.
- **Immutable & auditable:** every model/record/event is a `frozen` dataclass (runtime
  `FrozenInstanceError`); repositories are append-only; backward transitions create new lineage (RL-1).
- **Dependencies by identity only** (`dependencies` module) — no reaching into other contexts (SE-2),
  no isolation-barrier-breaching channel.
- **Compiles and imports cleanly**, 14 modules, no circular dependencies.
